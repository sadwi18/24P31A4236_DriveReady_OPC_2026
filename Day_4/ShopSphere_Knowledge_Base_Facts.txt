7. Standard Shipping Policy: Standard delivery across India takes 3 to 5 business days after order confirmation and dispatch.

11. Same-Day & Express Delivery: Same-day or 2-hour express delivery is not guaranteed automatically; requests for urgent city delivery must be escalated to the support team.
12. Accepted Payment Methods: Customers can pay online via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit cards, credit cards, net banking, or Cash on Delivery (COD).
13. Cash on Delivery (COD) Limit: Cash on Delivery is available across serviceable Indian PIN codes for orders up to Rs. 10,000 with zero extra COD fee.
14. Return Window Policy: Unused products in their original packaging with all tags and accessories can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
15. Non-Returnable Items: Used items, physically damaged products, or items missing original serial boxes and accessories are not eligible for return.
16. Refund Timeline: Once a returned item passes quality inspection at our warehouse, prepaid refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, and COD refunds are transferred via UPI or bank NEFT within 5 business days.
17. Order Cancellation Policy: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched from the warehouse; once shipped, customers can refuse delivery or initiate a return within 7 days.
18. Invoice & GST Billing: Every ShopSphere order includes a digital GST-compliant tax invoice sent to the customer's registered email upon dispatch.
